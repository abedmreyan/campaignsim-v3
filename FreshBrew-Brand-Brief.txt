FRESHBREW COFFEE — BRAND & CAMPAIGN BRIEF
Prepared for: Marketing Campaign Simulation
Version: 1.0

=== BRAND OVERVIEW ===

FreshBrew Coffee is a direct-to-consumer specialty coffee brand founded in 2021. We source
single-origin beans from Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala, and roast in small batches at our
facility in Austin, Texas. Our core promise is "coffee that respects your body and the planet."

Brand values: Sustainability, Transparency, Wellness, Craft
Price positioning: Premium (subscription boxes at $28–$45/month)
Distribution: 100% DTC via website and app. No retail presence.
Annual revenue: $4.2M (2025), growing 38% YoY.

=== PRODUCTS ===

1. Morning Ritual Blend — Light roast, low-acid, designed for people with sensitive stomachs.
   Positioning: The healthy morning coffee. Tagline: "Start clean."

2. Focus Fuel Espresso — Dark roast with added adaptogens (ashwagandha, lion's mane).
   Positioning: Coffee that works with your brain. Tagline: "Think clearer."

3. Cold Brew Concentrate — Ready-to-drink cold brew, 2x concentrate, no sugar added.
   Positioning: Grab-and-go clean energy. Tagline: "Fast. Clean. Cold."

=== TARGET AUDIENCE SEGMENTS ===

Segment 1 — Health-Conscious Millennials (Primary)
Age: 26–38. Income: $65k–$110k. Location: Urban, tier-1 cities.
Psychographics: Track macros, buy organic, follow wellness influencers, use Whoop/Oura.
Platforms: Instagram (daily), TikTok (3–5x/week), Spotify (heavy user).
Pain points: Existing coffee makes them anxious or crash mid-morning. Want clean energy.
Buying behaviour: Research-heavy. Read ingredient lists. Value brand transparency.
Ad response: Skip pre-roll. Engage with UGC and founder-led content. Distrust polished ads.

Segment 2 — Coffee Connoisseurs (Secondary)
Age: 30–55. Income: $85k–$150k. Location: Suburban, mixed.
Psychographics: Own a pour-over kit, visit specialty cafes, subscribe to coffee media.
Platforms: Email (high open rates), Reddit (r/coffee, r/espresso), YouTube.
Pain points: Subscription boxes deliver inconsistent quality. Want provenance story.
Buying behaviour: Loyal once converted. Will pay a premium for verifiable single-origin.
Ad response: Long-form content. Detailed origin stories. Peer recommendations on Reddit.

Segment 3 — Casual Upgraders (Tertiary)
Age: 22–45. Income: $40k–$75k. Location: Mixed.
Psychographics: Drink coffee daily but not invested in it as a hobby or lifestyle.
Platforms: Facebook, YouTube pre-roll, Google search.
Pain points: Current coffee is "fine" but they want something better without effort.
Buying behaviour: Price-sensitive. Need a strong CTA and discount to convert.
Ad response: Respond to promotions, discounts, and convenience messaging.

=== COMPETITORS ===

1. Atlas Coffee Club — Subscription model, strong origin storytelling. Weakness: No wellness angle.
2. Trade Coffee — Large SKU library, personalization quiz. Weakness: Overwhelming choice.
3. Starbucks at Home — Mass market, high brand recognition. Weakness: No DTC relationship.
4. Bulletproof Coffee — Wellness angle, similar price point. Direct competitor for Segment 1.

=== MARKETING CHANNELS IN USE ===

Instagram: Primary channel. Reels + Stories. 42k followers. Avg 4.2% engagement rate.
Email: 28k subscribers. 34% open rate (above industry avg). High revenue per email.
TikTok: Emerging. 8k followers. Testing UGC partnerships.
Google Ads: Search (brand + category). CPA $18.40.
Influencer partnerships: 12 active micro-influencers (10k–80k followers) in wellness space.

=== CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVE ===

Objective: Drive trial subscriptions for Morning Ritual Blend among Segment 1 (Health-Conscious
Millennials) and Segment 2 (Coffee Connoisseurs).

Target metric: 500 new trial subscriptions within 30 days of campaign launch.
Budget: $35,000 across all channels.

Campaign message pillars:
1. Clean energy without the crash (wellness angle)
2. Know your bean (provenance/transparency angle)
3. Better mornings start here (ritual/lifestyle angle)

=== CAMPAIGN VARIANTS TO TEST ===

VARIANT A — "Wellness Story" (Instagram Reels, 15-second video)
Headline: Your coffee shouldn't make you anxious.
Body: FreshBrew Morning Ritual — low-acid, no jitters, just clean energy. Try your first box free.
CTA: Start your ritual
Target segment: Health-Conscious Millennials (Segment 1)
Channel: Instagram
Format: Short video / Reel

VARIANT B — "Cafe Culture" (Email Newsletter, long-form)
Headline: From a farm in Huila, Colombia to your morning cup.
Body: 600-word story about the Huila cooperative farmers, the roasting process, and what
single-origin actually means. Includes high-res photography and a tasting notes section.
CTA: Subscribe and taste the difference
Target segment: Coffee Connoisseurs (Segment 2)
Channel: Email
Format: Long-form HTML newsletter

=== BRAND VOICE ===

Tone: Honest, direct, warm. Not preachy about wellness. Not corporate.
We say: "Your coffee shouldn't make you anxious."
We don't say: "Harness the synergistic bioavailability of our adaptogenic blend."
Visual identity: Clean whites, earth tones, photography-first. No stock photography.

=== AUDIENCE-CHANNEL FIT (Reference for Simulation) ===

Health-Conscious Millennials respond best to:
- Short-form video on Instagram and TikTok
- UGC and founder-led content
- Wellness and lifestyle framing
- Micro-influencer recommendations from trusted voices

Coffee Connoisseurs respond best to:
- Long-form written content about origin and craft
- Email newsletters with detailed tasting notes
- Reddit discussions and peer reviews
- YouTube deep-dives on coffee culture

Casual Upgraders respond best to:
- Promotional offers and discounts
- Facebook and Google display ads
- Convenience and value messaging
- Easy subscription with a strong free-trial hook
