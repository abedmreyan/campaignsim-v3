OLD SPICE "THE MAN YOUR MAN COULD SMELL LIKE" — CAMPAIGN BRIEF
Prepared for: Marketing Campaign Simulation Validation
Source: Published case studies (Wieden+Kennedy, D&AD Awards case study, Ad Age, Kinesis, marketing press coverage, 2010)

=== BRAND OVERVIEW ===

Old Spice is a long-running men's grooming brand (body wash, deodorant, antiperspirant) that, by the late 2000s, had an aging brand image associated with older generations. In 2010, working with agency Wieden+Kennedy, Old Spice repositioned its Red Zone body wash line toward a younger male demographic while deliberately speaking to the women who often purchase grooming products for the household.

Brand values (post-repositioning): Confidence, charm, self-aware absurdity, masculinity without machismo.
Market positioning: Old Spice is no longer "your dad's brand" — it's bold, funny, and impossible to ignore.

=== THE CAMPAIGN ===

The campaign launched in two connected phases:
1. A 30-second TV and digital spot, "The Man Your Man Could Smell Like," featuring actor Isaiah Mustafa delivering a rapid-fire, visually surreal direct-to-camera monologue aimed at women, about the kind of man their man could smell like if he used Old Spice.
2. "The Old Spice Response Campaign" — a follow-up where Isaiah Mustafa, in character, filmed and posted real-time personalized video responses to questions and comments from fans, journalists, and celebrities on Twitter and Facebook, turning the campaign into a two-way, interactive event.

=== TARGET AUDIENCE SEGMENTS ===

Segment 1 — The Household Purchaser
Age: 25–45 (primarily women).
Psychographics: Buys grooming products for their household/partner, active on social media, enjoys sharing funny ads with friends.
Platforms: Facebook, daytime TV, YouTube (via shared links).
Buying behaviour: Influences or directly makes the purchase decision for their partner's grooming products.
Ad response: Highly responsive to humor, charm, and ads that are "shareable" — content they want to send to friends, not just watch.

Segment 2 — The Online Culture Participant
Age: 18–34.
Psychographics: Heavy social media user, follows internet culture and viral trends closely, enjoys being part of a real-time cultural moment.
Platforms: Twitter, YouTube, Reddit, Facebook.
Buying behaviour: Not necessarily the direct purchaser, but drives awareness and amplification through sharing, commenting, and creating derivative content/memes.
Ad response: Responds strongly to novelty, interactivity, and content that reacts to them personally (e.g., a brand directly responding to their tweet).

=== CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVE ===

Objective: Reverse Old Spice's declining relevance among younger consumers and reposition Red Zone Body Wash as the leading men's body wash brand, while driving a measurable lift in body wash sales.

Context: The original internal sales target for the campaign was modest. The campaign's actual mandate was to generate outsized cultural attention relative to a normal body-wash ad budget.

Target metric: Body wash sales growth (year-over-year) and online video engagement (views, social follower growth) during the campaign window.

=== CAMPAIGN VARIANTS TO TEST ===

Note: the original 2010 media plan ran on TV, YouTube, Twitter, and Facebook —
platforms that predate or barely overlap with today's short-form social stack.
The two variants below adapt the same creative concepts onto the channels this
simulation actually models, as the modern equivalent placements a brand would
choose to run this exact creative today.

VARIANT A — "The Original Spot" (polished one-way video push)
Headline: The Man Your Man Could Smell Like.
Body: A single uninterrupted direct-to-camera monologue by a confident, charming man, moving through a series of surreal visual gags (boat, shower, hot air balloon, back to boat), aimed at the woman watching, ending with "I'm on a horse."
CTA: (implicit) Buy Old Spice for your man.
Target segment: Household Purchaser (Segment 1)
Channel: Instagram
Format: VideoAd

VARIANT B — "The Response Campaign" (real-time interactive follow-up)
Headline: Old Spice Guy is responding to YOU, right now.
Body: Isaiah Mustafa, in character, films and posts individual short video replies to real questions and comments from fans, reacting within hours of the original post.
CTA: Ask Old Spice Guy a question.
Target segment: Online Culture Participant (Segment 2)
Channel: TikTok
Format: ShortFormVideo

=== BRAND VOICE ===

Tone: Confident, absurd, self-aware, fast-paced. Never takes itself seriously.
We say things that are deliberately illogical and theatrical, owning the absurdity rather than explaining it.
